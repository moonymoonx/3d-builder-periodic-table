**Summary:** I checked **47** factual, scientific, data-provenance, and licensing claims across `BRIEF.md`, `SPEC.md`, `RESEARCH.md`, `PLAN.md`, and the repository's raw/compiled data files: **26 correct**, **18 wrong**, and **3 unverifiable** (the X-post metrics and uninspected UI details of `sael.net/periodic-table`, whose HTML header carries an explicit automated-reader/all-rights-reserved notice). **Top 5 things to fix first:** (1) **Licensing blockers for a single public HTML file** — NIST ASD (SRD 78) and CODATA (SRD 121) carry U.S. Secretary of Commerce copyright under 15 U.S.C. § 290e ("All rights reserved... without prior permission"), IAEA LiveChart has non-open Terms of Use (switch to published NUBASE2020 CC BY 3.0), and embedding `CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv` (CC BY-SA 4.0) or Wikipedia notes triggers ShareAlike obligations; (2) **Broken/suboptimal COD CIF picks & missing lattices** — `cod/1527120.cif` is cryogenic α-U (use `9008584`), `cod/7222459.cif` is nanocrystalline battery-electrode α-Sn (use `9008568`), `cod/9008562.cif` is non-standard `Bmeb` α-Ga (prefer `9008085` `Cmce`), Ra has Wikipedia's `cF4` typo instead of `cI2`, Db is misclassified `nodata` instead of `predicted`, and Cm/Bk/Cf/Es lack lattice constants (currently 80 lattices built instead of 84); (3) **Physics bugs in `build-data.mjs` / `placeholders.json`** — `r0 = 1.2 fm` mixes uniform-sphere radius with IAEA rms charge radii (~29% overestimate; use `r0 ≈ 0.94 fm` for rms), gas `v_rms = √(3RT/M)` must use molecular molar mass `2×A_r` for diatomic gases (H₂, N₂, O₂, F₂, Cl₂), and Ce's room-temperature equilibrium/default phase needs careful handling (γ-Ce FCC vs β-Ce dhcp); (4) **PubChem data errors leaking into `elements.json`** — outdated/wrong boiling/melting points (Ga, Ba, Ra, Bk), discovery years (Al, Ca, F, Si, Ru), Og predicted as gas instead of solid, and `Lr` configuration `[Rn]7s2 5f14 6d1` (NIST ASD fixes this to `7p1`, but NIST neutral IEs stop at Z=108); (5) **Vietnamese GDPT-2018 naming & `sael.net` copyright notice** — `build-data.mjs` reverses `SPEC.md §3` by making `natri` primary over `sodium` for the 13 traditional names, and `sael.net/periodic-table` explicitly forbids copying its layout/visual style without Ryan Sael's permission.

## Data files: issues found (actionable fixes for Claude's build pipeline)

1. **`tools/lib/cod-select.mjs` — Bad CIF picks for U, α-Sn, and Ga:**
   - *Cause:* `codCandidates()` does not filter titles containing `low temperature` or `nanocrystalline`, and `rankCod()` breaks ties alphabetically (`"1527120" < "9008584"`) or favors newer `Rall`-bearing nanoparticle refinements (`7222459` over bulk `9008568`).
   - *Fix:* Filter out `/low temperature|nanocryst|electrode|thin film/i` in `codCandidates()` (or pin preferred bulk room-T COD IDs: U → `9008584`, α-Sn → `9008568`, α-Ga → `9008085` in standard `Cmce`).
2. **`data/curated/placeholders.json` & `tools/build-data.mjs` (line 414) — Nuclear radius mismatch (`r0 = 1.2 fm`):**
   - *Cause:* IAEA `radius` is the **rms charge radius** (`R_rms`), whereas `1.2·A^(1/3) fm` is the **equivalent uniform-sphere radius** (`R_equiv = √(5/3)·R_rms`).
   - *Fix:* Either change the fallback to `r0_rms = 0.94 fm` (with `R_rms ≈ 0.94·A^(1/3)` fm, or `√(3/5) × 1.2 ≈ 0.93 fm`) OR convert IAEA `R_rms` to equivalent hard-sphere radius `R_equiv = √(5/3)·R_rms` everywhere consistently.
3. **`data/elements.json` / `tools/build-data.mjs` — Ra (`cF4`), Th (`facecentredcubic`), Cs (`bodycentredcubic`), Db (`nodata`), Cm/Bk/Cf/Es (`nodata`), Ce default:**
   - *Ra (Z=88):* Wikipedia's `Template:Infobox radium` has a typo `cF4` alongside `body-centered cubic`. Override Pearson symbol to **`cI2`** (space group `Im-3m`, #229, `a = 514.8 pm`, Weigel & Trinkl 1968 / Arblaster 2018).
   - *Th (Z=90) & Cs (Z=55):* Normalize stripped wikitext names `facecentredcubic` → `face-centered cubic` and `bodycentredcubic` → `body-centered cubic`.
   - *Db (Z=105):* `build-data.mjs` classifies Db as `"kind":"nodata"` instead of `"kind":"predicted"` because Wikipedia omitted `(predicted)` on the `crystal structure` line (it only put `(predicted)` on `phase`). Force `Z >= 100` (all unmeasured bulk structures) to `"kind":"predicted"`.
   - *Cm (96), Bk (97), Cf (98), Es (99):* Add measured room-temperature lattice constants from the literature (see Gaps Filled table below) to reach all **84** measured ≤16-atom lattices.
   - *Ce (Z=58):* At 298 K and 1 atm, **γ-Ce (FCC, `cF4`, `a = 516.10 pm`)** is the standard commercially observed phase (stable on cooling to ~263 K with large hysteresis), while **β-Ce (dhcp, `hP4`, `a = 368.11, c = 1185.7 pm`)** is the thermodynamic equilibrium phase near/below room temperature (~123–348 K depending on evaluation). Making **γ-Ce** default (or clearly explaining the β/γ room-T coexistence) avoids surprising users.
